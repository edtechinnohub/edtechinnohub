Too many people can access education only in theory — while lacking the practical knowledge, digital skills, tools and opportunities needed to use what they've learned in real life. We've seen this play out differently across the people we work with: children whose learning feels disconnected from everyday life, young people with potential but no practical digital skills, teachers who are experts in their subject but unsure how to use technology in the classroom, and communities where internet access, electricity, or cost make digital education hard to reach at all.

EdTech InnoHub exists to help close these gaps.

## Our approach

We think about this in five steps: **Learn → Practise → Create → Earn → Impact.**

A young person shouldn't just learn data analysis — they should use it to solve a real problem. A teacher shouldn't just attend an AI workshop — they should walk out able to use AI to prepare a better lesson. A child shouldn't just memorise a science concept — they should recognise it in the world around them.

## Where we are

We're early-stage and founder-led — building and validating our first products and programmes, one at a time. This site is where that work will live: our books, our training, our research, and honest updates on what we're learning along the way.
