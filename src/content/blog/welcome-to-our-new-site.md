For a while, our site was just a placeholder — a way for people to find us and read about our mission. Today, it becomes something more useful: a real home for the books, guides, and stories that come out of our work.

## What's new

- A **Books** section where every title is free to download, no request forms or email gates.
- A **Resources** library for guides, worksheets, and webinar recordings.
- This **Blog**, where we'll share updates from our programs and lessons from the field.

## Why it matters

Our mission is bridging the education gap through inclusive, equitable, and quality learning — and that means the materials we create should be as easy to reach as possible. This site is built so that new books and resources appear here automatically as soon as we add them to our library, without anyone having to touch a line of code.

More is coming soon. Thank you for being part of this.
